What We Offer

Everything You Need

A complete toolkit for personalized beauty recommendations, powered by AI and designed for every Filipino complexion.

AI Skin Analysis

Detect and analyze skin characteristics from facial images using MediaPipe facial landmark detection and OpenCV image processing.

Personalized Color Palette

Generate foundation, blush, lip, and eyeshadow colors based on your detected skin tone and skin type for a truly tailored look.

Webcam Capture

Use your device's camera for a quick, real-time selfie capture with face positioning guidance and lighting indicators.

Image Upload

Upload a clear frontal facial image from your device. Supports JPG and PNG formats with drag-and-drop convenience.

Recommendation History

Registered users can view all previous analyses and color palettes, track changes over time, and revisit past results.

Simple & Accessible

A fully responsive interface designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile — easy to navigate for all users regardless of tech background.
