Photo Tips
  • Face the camera directly, looking straight ahead.
  • Use natural or soft indoor lighting — avoid harsh shadows.
  • Remove glasses, heavy accessories, or face coverings.
  • Keep your face clean or lightly made up for best results.
  • Ensure your entire face is clearly visible in the frame.
  • Avoid strong backlighting or flash directly on the face.
  • Do not upload images where face is obscured or at an angle.
